# SceneGap Edge: business and competition facts

All sources accessed 2026-10-26. "Verified" key:

- **yes**: the number or statement was read on the cited page itself.
- **partial**: the cited page is the official/primary source, but our fetch was blocked (HTTP 403/404/JS); the number comes from that page's search-engine excerpt or from the same figure repeated by several secondary outlets.
- **no**: secondary, dated, vendor marketing with no method, or conflicting sources. Do not put on a slide without a caveat.

Vendor claims (speed, accuracy, "world's first") are the vendor's words, not independent tests.
